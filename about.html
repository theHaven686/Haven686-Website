Our Story

God's story.

We didn't arrive at Haven 686 through strategy or ambition. God has been writing this story longer than we have — preparing us quietly, over more than a decade, for exactly this.

We met in 2012 in a prayer group at Pensacola Christian College — Boone studying Graphic Design, Cali studying Studio Art. While in college, we both served in ministry capacities — Cali as an RA and Boone leading a campus prayer group and visiting boys in juvenile detention weekly. We also spent our summers working as camp counselors where a deep love for these kids steadily grew in our hearts.

We spent more than seven years at Whetstone Boys Ranch in Missouri, serving at-risk teenage boys. Boone rose from direct care staff to House Manager, overseeing hiring, training, and crisis intervention, while Cali taught art and poured herself into the boys in every way she could. We opened our home to them constantly — family dinners, big game nights, countless quiet teaching moments. We learned what it means to be a family to kids who don't have one.

In 2025, we became licensed foster parents and immediately began fostering two older boys. What we learned in this season was crucial — you could even call it a crucible — for the development of Haven. Stepping into fostering immediately began to hone our vision, teaching us invaluable lessons to apply to the Haven.

That same year, after more than a decade of chronic illness, God healed Cali. The very week of her healing, He put the vision for Haven 686 on our hearts — clear, complete, and unmistakably His.

We said yes.

This is not our mission. We inherited it from Scripture and are doing our best to carry it faithfully.

Our Mission

To provide a purpose-built community and homes that enable Christian foster parents to care for children in need — removing barriers to fostering and helping every child achieve permanency when returning home is not an option. Visit our campus page.

Our Vision

The campus isn't the ceiling. Tennessee has over 8,000 children in foster care and roughly 9,000 Christian churches. Our long-term vision is a statewide movement — enough loving homes for every child in need. Visit our churches page.

Our Values

Any spiritual endeavor is bound to failure if it stands upon man's wisdom. A deep and abiding faith in Christ — his total ability to provide and uphold his people and the work he gives them — is the first and foremost value of Haven. We will go forward in unified faith.

"Without faith, it is impossible to please Him."— Hebrews 11

God has called us to this ministry, and from His throne alone we will seek wisdom to carry it on. All decisions of consequence are made only after seeking the face of God in prayer, going forward only when the Spirit of God has given peace and unity for the decision. All members of the board are expected to bring this same prayerful attitude to every decision.

"If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God, who gives generously to all without reproach, and it will be given him."— James 1:5

"Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths."— Proverbs 3:5–6

"But seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you."— Matthew 6:33

"I will instruct you and teach you in the way you should go; I will counsel you with my eye upon you."— Psalm 32:8

Given the nature of the work we seek to accomplish in caring for the "orphans," valuing the biblical structure of family is imperative. A family, at best, operates according to the roles established in Ephesians 5 and 6. While a traditional family structure isn't required of everyone who serves at Haven, it is the ideal setting for a child to be trained up within. What we value is the totality of Christ-likeness found within a godly family functioning properly in accordance with biblical instruction — agape love (1 Corinthians 13), biblical unity (1 Peter 3:8), encouragement (Ephesians 4:15–16), and God-ordained roles (Ephesians 5:22–6:4).

"Finally, all of you, have unity of mind, sympathy, brotherly love, a tender heart, and a humble mind."— 1 Peter 3:8

A major part of Haven will be the strength, encouragement, compassion, and love experienced within this Christian community. A theme throughout Scripture is that Christians should love one another just as Christ has loved us — and that kind of love requires the framework of community to thrive within. It's a beautiful thing when brothers and sisters in Christ thrive in unified community together.

"Two are better than one, because they have a good reward for their toil. For if they fall, one will lift up his fellow... a threefold cord is not quickly broken."— Ecclesiastes 4:9–12

"Let us consider how to stir up one another to love and good works, not neglecting to meet together... encouraging one another, and all the more as you see the Day drawing near."— Hebrews 10:24–25

There is a true wonder that God has imbued in nature. It's hard to describe, but easy to feel. Nature, at rest, is a place that brings healing, closeness to God, and perspective to the busy, man-made life we often find ourselves operating within. We believe nature in its various forms should remain integral to Haven's operation as a God-given tool for healing, rest, joy, peace, curiosity, love, knowledge, and perspective.

"But ask the beasts, and they will teach you... in his hand is the life of every living thing and the breath of all mankind."— Job 12:7–10

"He has made everything beautiful in its time. Also, he has put eternity into man's heart, yet so that he cannot find out what God has done from the beginning to the end."— Ecclesiastes 3:11

Religion that is pure and undefiled before God the Father is this:

to visit orphans and widows in their affliction.

— James 1:27
