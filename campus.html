The Haven Campus

A village built
for foster care.

Rent-free homes, shared land, and a Christian community designed to make long-term foster care sustainable — for families ready to commit.

The Model

More than a home.
A community.

The Haven campus will be a shared property with multiple independent homes where Christian foster families live rent-free — each family its own household, free to foster through the licensing agency of their choice. What they share is the land, the community, and other families doing the same work.

Haven 686 provides and maintains the homes. House parents provide the care. No rent. No mortgage. The financial barrier — one of the most cited reasons families never start or eventually quit — removed entirely.

House Parents

We're looking for families
who are ready.

Haven 686 homes are for Christian foster families called to this work and ready for full commitment. House parents are vetted through a rigorous process — applications, interviews, background checks, pastoral references, and a signed statement of faith.

Adoption is encouraged when appropriate. Families who reach adoption capacity may stay up to three years before the home passes to a new foster family.

There's no fixed placement quota — one home might serve a single high-need child, another a sibling group of five. What we ask is full engagement: as many children as a household can excellently care for.

The Property

Land that does more
than hold houses.

Each home will sit on roughly one acre, with access to shared land — a community garden, recreational space, and woods. We're prayerfully seeking the property now.

Haven 686 will remain debt-free throughout development. Land is purchased only when funded. Homes are built one at a time, as resources allow.

Future expansions may include small-scale farming, equine-assisted therapy, and a dedicated private school — not additions for their own sake, but tools for healing built into the land itself.
