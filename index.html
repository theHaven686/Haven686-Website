The Reality

42% of foster youth

will be incarcerated at least once by the time they turn 20.

It Doesn't Have to Stay This Way

Foster families that provide stability, identity, and "family" are usually the story behind the other 58%. Together we can create better outcomes for these kids.

The Reality

40% of foster families quit

within the first year. Isolation and lack of support are the most cited reasons.

It Doesn't Have to Stay This Way

The 60% who don't quit are almost never doing it alone — they have a church or community wrapped around them. That's exactly what Haven exists to provide, so more families can stay the course.

The Reality

9% of kids

who leave foster care age out completely alone — no family, no home, just a birthday and a system that stops.

It Doesn't Have to Stay This Way

A family who says yes — through adoption or guardianship — is the reason a child never has to find out what aging out alone feels like.
